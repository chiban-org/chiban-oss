# Roles and permissions

Use this page when you set up a staff account and need to know which role gives a person what they need, or when someone asks why a page or a button is missing for them. For what each role is for, start with [how the staff roles fit together](../start-here/staff-roles.md).

A person with more than one role has every permission of each. Roles come with these permissions as shipped, and there is no settings page that changes them.

## Permissions

Each row is one thing Chiban checks before it lets a person act. The second column lists the roles that have it. Reviewers and board members hold none of these permissions. They reach their pages through the role itself.

## Pages each role sees

These are the pages in the staff menu, and the pages under **Settings**, for a person who holds only that one role. A person with two roles sees the pages of both. Data export, moving open applications over and scanned property files appear under **Settings** for an administrator and in the staff menu for anyone else who can use them.
