# New Hampshire starters

A starter is a ready-made application type. Adopting one copies a complete permit into your settings: its form, its fee schedule, its conditions, its inspection checklists, the letters and permit it prints, and the deadlines state law attaches to it. Use this page to decide which starters your town needs before you adopt them, and to see what each one asks of an applicant.

## Before you adopt

The fees in every starter are examples written for the fictional Town of Alder Pond. Change each amount to your own fee schedule before you take a real application.

Adopting publishes the new application type right away, so it is live as soon as you adopt it. Set its fees, form and letters under **Settings** before you tell the public it is there.

A later release of Chiban can improve a starter you have already adopted. **Check adopted starters for updates**, at the foot of the **Starter templates** page, lists the changes. Tick the ones you want, and everything you leave unticked stays as your town has it.

Adopting a starter needs the permission to edit configuration, which administrators have.

## How to adopt a starter

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Starter templates**.
2. Find the starter under **Ready to adopt** and choose **Adopt this starter**.
3. Open **Settings**, **Fee schedules** and set your town's amounts.
4. Read through the form under **Settings**, **Forms** and the letters under **Settings**, **Templates**, and change anything that does not match how your office works.

## How to read the catalog

Each entry gives the type of proceeding, who decides it, and the letters its application numbers start with. Then it lists:

- **Fees:** each fee line as a clerk would quote it, and when it falls due. "Due with notice" fees are charged when staff issue the abutter list, which is when the number of abutters is known.
- **Documents the applicant provides:** what the applicant must hand in, and when. "To file" documents are needed before the application can be submitted. "When it applies" means the form asks a question first, and the document is needed only for some answers.
- **Inspections:** the inspection checklists that come with the permit.
- **Deadlines Chiban tracks:** the state and town deadlines that start on applications of this type. Each one is explained on [deadlines and holidays](./deadlines.mdx).
