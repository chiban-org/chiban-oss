## Building permits

### Building permit decision deadline

- **How long:** 30 days after the application is complete; 60 days when the work is nonresidential; 60 days when there are more than 10 dwelling units.
- **Who acts:** Town staff.
- **Counting:** Calendar days: the start day is not counted and the last day is (RSA 21:35 I). It does not move for weekends or holidays.
- **If it is missed:** RSA 155-A:4 IX states no consequence for a missed deadline. The application shows as overdue.
- **Extension:** RSA 155-A:4 IX provides no extension.
- **Source:** [RSA 155-A:4 IX](https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XII/155-A/155-A-4.htm)

### Regional impact comment period

- **How long:** 30 days after the regional impact notice is mailed.
- **Who acts:** Someone outside the town office.
- **Counting:** Calendar days: the start day is not counted and the last day is (RSA 21:35 I). A last day that falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday moves to the next business day (RSA 21:35 II).
- **If it is missed:** The permit may not issue until the comment period ends.
- **Extension:** RSA 36:57 IV provides no extension.
- **Source:** [RSA 36:57 IV](https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/III/36/36-57.htm)

### Building permit exercise-by date

- **Counting:** Calendar months: the start day is not counted and the last day is (RSA 21:35 I).

## Right-to-Know requests
