## Building permits

### Building permit decision deadline

- **How long:** 30 days after the application is received.
- **Who acts:** Town staff.
- **Counting:** Calendar days: the start day is not counted and the last day is (M.R. Civ. P. 6(a); 1 MRSA §71(12)). It does not move for weekends or holidays.
- **If it is missed:** Under 30-A MRSA §4103(3), no written decision to the applicant within 30 days of filing is a refusal of the permit.
- **Extension:** 30-A MRSA §4103(3) provides no extension.
- **Source:** [30-A MRSA §4103(3)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec4103.html)

### Building permit exercise-by date

- **How long:** The town’s building permit validity period (Chiban starting value), 12 months unless the town changes it, after the permit is issued.
- **Who acts:** The applicant.
- **Counting:** Calendar months: the start day is not counted and the last day is (M.R. Civ. P. 6(a); 1 MRSA §71(12)).
- **If it is missed:** The permit expires unless renewed.
- **Extension:** May be extended by renewal.
- **Source:** Town rule
