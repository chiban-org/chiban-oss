# Assigning reviewers

Assigning a reviewer asks someone outside the land use office to look at an application. This page is for clerks. [Reviewing an application](./index.md) describes what the reviewer then sees.

## Who can be a reviewer

Anyone who holds the **Reviewer** role and has an active account. An administrator gives people the role under **Settings**, **Users**. If the person you want does not appear in the list, they do not hold the role yet. [How the staff roles fit together](../../start-here/staff-roles.md) describes it.

Land use staff and administrators can assign and remove reviewers.

## Assign a reviewer

You can do it from three places.

- On the application record, in **Do this now** when the step is **Assign a reviewer**. The **Reviewer** list shows everyone who is not already assigned. Choose a name and **Assign reviewer**.
- In the **Reviewers** panel on the record, open **Assign a reviewer**. The panel also lists everyone assigned, with their status.
- In the **Applications** list, choose **Unassigned** in the **Reviewers** column. The **Assign a reviewer** box opens.

Chiban answers **Reviewer assigned.** The reviewer is emailed that a review has been requested. If you assign someone who is already on the application, Chiban says **That reviewer is already assigned.**

Assigning a reviewer does not share any documents with them. Open **Change who can see this** on each document the reviewer needs and tick **Assigned reviewers**, then choose **Save signed-in access**. A document the applicant uploaded may already be shared with reviewers, as the line under it says: **Who can see this**, followed by who.

## Remove a reviewer

In the **Reviewers** panel, open **Remove** beside the name and confirm. The person no longer sees the application.

## What reviews do to a decision

On a permit that goes through review to a decision, such as a building permit, the requirement **Reviews signed off** blocks **Record the decision** until at least one reviewer is assigned and every assigned reviewer has signed off.

- With nobody assigned, the requirement says **No reviewer is assigned.** and the panel shows **The decision is blocked until a reviewer is assigned and signs off.**
- With reviewers who have not finished, it says how many reviews are not signed off.

If a reviewer is away or no longer needed, **Remove** them. Removing is better than overriding the requirement, which ignores the reviews altogether. Land use staff can override with a recorded reason, as [From intake to issued permit](../land-use-staff/application-life.md) explains.

Board applications are different. For these the requirement is not used. Review comes from the board.

## Asking a board for comment

On the same panel, **Send to a board for comment** asks a board, for example the Conservation Commission, to comment on an application it does not decide. Choose the board and, if you wish, a comment-by date. The members seated on the board are emailed, and the panel lists the board under **Boards asked for comment** with **Waiting for comments** or **Comments received**. **Withdraw** takes the request back. A board's comment does not block the decision, and a board member does not sign off the way a reviewer does.

## When the review is back

**Reviews back, ready to decide** on **Today** counts applications where the reviewers have finished. The **Reviews back** flag on the **Applications** list finds them too.
