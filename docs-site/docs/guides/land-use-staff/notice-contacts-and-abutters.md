# Notice contacts and abutter lists

When a board application needs mailed notice, the notice goes to the abutters of the lot. For most parcels that is the owner of record on the assessing file. A few kinds of parcel are different: a condominium's notice goes to the association's officers, a manufactured housing park's goes to the park owner, and a lot under a conservation restriction also notifies the holder. **Notice contacts** is where you keep those people, and an abutter list uses them when it is drawn.

## Notice contacts

Open **Notice contacts**. The tabs sort the contacts by kind:

- **Associations** are condominium associations. Notices for a condo parcel go to the association officers, not the unit owners.
- **Parks** are manufactured housing parks. Notices go to the park owner. Each tenant is listed, and you decide for each application whether the tenant's home is adjoining or across.
- **Restriction holders** are holders of conservation and preservation restrictions. When an applicant's land carries one of these, the holder is notified.
- **Missing association** lists condo parcels with no association on file. An abutter list that touches one of these cannot be issued until its association is added.
- **Regional and state** lists the regional and state contacts used when an application has regional impact. Administrators change these under **Settings**.

![The Notice contacts page, showing the due checks and the tab for each kind of contact](/img/shots/land-use-staff/notice-contacts.png)

Each tab has a search box (**Search name, parcel ID or address**), a status filter (**Due for a check**, **Active**, **Ended**) and an add button: **Add association**, **Add park** or **Add holder**. Choose the parcel the contact belongs to and fill in the name and the mailing address. An association also takes the date its list is current as of. A row's **Edit** changes it, and **End** retires it with an end date, which **Undo ending** reverses.

### Keeping contacts current

Each contact has a **Checked** date. Choose **Confirm still correct** when you have checked a contact against what you know, and the date moves. A contact is **Due for a check** when its date is older than the interval shown on the page. Alder Pond's is 365 days, and an administrator can change it. A contact past the interval does not stop a list from issuing. The printed list marks that contact as out of date.

For restriction holders there is also **Record a check** next to the line saying when the land records were last checked, so the office can show that it looked.

## Abutter lists

An abutter list belongs to a board application. You draw it from the application's record, and the [hearings page](../boards/hearings.md) covers mailing the notice.

1. Open the board application and find **Abutter lists** in the board panel. If the application needs a list, **Do this now** may say so, and once a draft exists it offers **Open version** and the draft's number **to issue it**.
2. Choose **Draw a new list**. Chiban reads the parcel, road and water layers, picks out the parcels that touch the lot or sit across a street or stream, and adds the people who must receive notice. It starts at **Version 1** as a draft.
3. Open the version to review it.

If the application involves more than one lot, use **Add a parcel to this application** first. If a surveyor, engineer, architect or soil scientist's seal is on the plat, use **Add a professional whose seal is on the plat**, because that person receives notice too.

### Reading a list

The page for a list has these parts, top to bottom:

- **Before this list can be issued** appears when something is missing, such as an association, with a link to fix it.
- **Across the street or stream** is a map that shows how Chiban found the parcels across a road or a stream. Every parcel on the map is also in the tables.
- **Parcels considered** lists each parcel, its owner on record, and why it is on the list, for example **Touches the property** or **Across Osprey Point Road, between the projected points**.
- **Who receives notice** lists each person with their mailing address, the role they receive notice as, such as **Abutting owner**, **Applicant**, **Easement or restriction holder** or **Professional whose seal is on the plat**, and where the information came from.
- **Where the data came from** says when each layer was last imported and whether it is out of date.

### Fixing a list

A draft is yours to change before it is issued.

- **Leave off** beside a parcel removes it from the list. A reason is required and is kept with the list.
- **Add a parcel across a street or stream** adds a parcel Chiban missed. Choose the parcel, whether it is across a **Street** or a **Stream**, and explain **Why it belongs on the list**.
- **Confirm streets and streams were reviewed** records that you checked the crossings.
- **Correct the name or address** changes a recipient's name or mailing address, with a reason.
- **Decide whether this tenant counts** records, for a park tenant, whether they own housing that adjoins or is directly across.

### Issuing and printing

When the list is right, choose **Issue list** and confirm. Issuing works out the cost of the mailed notice from the number of mailings and replaces any earlier issued list. It cannot be undone. A new list can be drawn later, which becomes the next version.

An issued list shows a **Check code** that matches every printed copy of that version. **Print mailing labels** offers two sheets, **Avery 5160** and **Avery 5163**, as PDF files.

The cost shows under **Notice cost** on the list and as a **Mailed notice to abutters** line on the application's fees. The applicant owes it, and it is paid like any other fee. See [Fees, waivers and payments](./fees-and-payments.md).

On a Planning Board application the list must be drawn within five days of filing, and the record's requirements say so. [Deadlines and holidays](../../state-packs/new-hampshire/deadlines.mdx) lists the notice rules Chiban tracks.

Land use staff and administrators keep the contacts, draw lists and issue them.
