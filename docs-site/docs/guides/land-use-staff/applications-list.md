# The Applications list

**Applications** is the office's work queue. Every application and permit in the town is on it, so open it when you need to know what to do next.

![The Applications list on the Needs action tab](/img/shots/land-use-staff/applications-list.png)

## The tabs

The tabs across the top group applications by stage, with a count on each.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs action** | Applications that are submitted or under review, plus any other application with a deadline running or overdue. Soonest due comes first. This is the default. |
| **Open** | Drafts, submitted, under review and decided. |
| **Issued or suspended** | Permits in force and permits that are suspended. |
| **Closed, denied, withdrawn or expired** | Everything that has ended. |
| **All** | All of the above, drafts included. |

## The filters

Under the tabs is a search box, **Search by number**, which matches part of an application number. Next to it are quick filters:

- **Overdue**, **Due this week** and **No deadline running** narrow by the nearest deadline.
- **Mine** and **Unassigned** narrow by responsibility. **Mine** counts applications you are responsible for or reviewing, and **Unassigned** counts those with neither.
- **Type** narrows to one or more application types.
- **Status** narrows to one or more stages, such as **Under review**.
- **Flags** picks out **Fees due**, **Payment outcome unknown**, **Preservation incomplete** and **Reviews back**.

A line under the filters says how many applications match, and **Clear all** removes the filters. The numbers on **Today** link straight here with a filter already set.

## The columns

Each row is one application:

- **Application** shows the number, the project title and the type. A chip reading **Payment outcome unknown** or **Preservation incomplete** appears when either applies.
- **Parcel** links to the parcel page.
- **Status** shows the stage, and a more specific step where the permit type has one, for example **Under review, hearing**.
- **Due** shows the next deadline and which rule it comes from. Overdue deadlines say how many days late, and a late notice is listed under the deadline.
- **Fees** shows the amount still due. A dash means nothing is due.
- **Reviewers** lists each reviewer with a state such as **Signed off** or **Pending**. For land use staff, **Unassigned** in this column is a button.
- **Responsible** is the staff member who owns the application, or **Nobody yet**.
- **Received** is the date it was filed.

Click a column heading to sort by it. The default sort is **Due**.

## Assign a reviewer from the list

Choose **Unassigned** in the **Reviewers** column. A box titled **Assign a reviewer** opens with the people who hold the reviewer role. Pick one and save. [Assigning reviewers](../reviewers/assigning.md) explains when to do this and what the reviewer receives.

## Open an application

Choose the application number. The record is described in [From intake to issued permit](./application-life.md).
