# Where to find each setting

Only administrators see **Settings** in the staff menu. It opens on a menu of seven groups, and each group opens to its pages. The **Filter settings** box at the top narrows the menu as you type, so you can type "fees" or "notice" instead of opening each group.

![The Settings page with its seven groups and the Town profile form open.](/img/shots/administrator/settings-menu.png)

Most pages in **Permits**, **Town rules**, **Boards** and **Money and inspections**, and the town pages, work the same way: you edit a draft, save it, and put it into use when it is ready. See [how changes are saved and put into use](./how-changes-are-put-into-use.md).

## The groups

| Group | Pages | What they decide | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Town | Town profile, Parcel ID formats, Letterhead, Signers, Notification wording | How the town's name, address and signers look on every document, and what emails say | [Town profile, letterhead and signers](./town-profile-letterhead-signers.md), [Wording of emails](./notification-wording.md), [Parcel ID formats](./parcel-id-formats.md) |
| Permits | Application types, Forms, Fee schedules, Templates, Conditions, Inspection checklists | What the town accepts, what applicants are asked, what it costs, what letters print | [Application types and workflow](./application-types-and-workflow.md), [Forms, fee schedules and letters](./forms-fee-schedules-and-letters.md) |
| Town rules | Town rule settings, Local code amendments, State law, Building code editions, Sections for findings | The values state law leaves to the town, local changes to the building code, and the sections staff cite in enforcement | [State law, town rules and local code](./town-rules-and-state-law.md) |
| Boards | Boards, Board settings, Regional notice contacts, Review committee routes | Which boards exist, how they give public notice, who gets regional notices, who reviews what | [Boards, notices and review committees](./boards-and-notices.md) |
| Money and inspections | Card payments, Inspection schedule | The payment processor, the convenience fee and receipts, and when inspections can be booked | [Card payments](./card-payments.md), [The inspection schedule](./inspection-schedule.md) |
| Starters | Starter templates | Ready-made permits and board applications, and updates to the ones adopted | [Adopting starters and taking updates](./adopting-starters.md) |
| Administration | Users, Enforcement officers, Anonymous intake, Data export, Backups, Health, Move open applications over, Scanned property files | Staff accounts, who may issue orders, whether the public can report anonymously, where the town's regular backups go, and the state of the system | [Inviting staff](./inviting-staff.md), [Enforcement officers](./enforcement-officers.md), [Anonymous complaints](./anonymous-intake.md), [The Health page](./health.md) |

The **Workflow** and **Report columns** pages do not appear in the menu. You reach them from an application type. See [application types and workflow](./application-types-and-workflow.md).

**Backups** sets where the town's encrypted backups are sent, how often, at what hour, and how many to keep, and shows the last result with a **Back up now** button. It needs storage set up under **Data export** first. The server side is covered in [backups and restore](../../operations/backups.md).

Three **Administration** pages belong to other guides. **Data export** is covered by [the export archive format](../../reports-and-export/export-format.md) and [monthly export to storage the town controls](../../reports-and-export/incremental-export.md). **Move open applications over** and **Scanned property files** bring records in from an old system. See [loading an old permit ledger](../../moving-in/spreadsheet-migration.md).

## Overrides are not in Settings

**Overrides** is in the staff menu for administrators and land use staff. It holds fee waivers, clock adjustments and parcel ID overrides. See [overrides](./overrides.md).

## If a page says you lack permission

The settings pages check that you can edit the town's configuration. Someone with the Land use staff role sees "Settings: none" in the menu. The [roles and permissions reference](../../reference/roles-and-permissions.mdx) lists the pages each role sees.
