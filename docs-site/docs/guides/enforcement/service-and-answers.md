# Serving an order, answers and deadlines

An order only counts once the right people have been served and the town can show it. Chiban keeps a checklist of who has to be served, records each service with its proof, and counts the answer period for each person from the day they were served.

![A case with an issued order waiting for service on the occupying tenant](/img/shots/enforcement/case-serve-order.png)

## Who has to be served

The order's service checklist covers these roles: **Owner of record**, **Owner agent**, **Taxpayer**, **Occupying tenant**, **Person in control of the property** and **Mortgagee of record**. For each role you either list the people to be served or record that there are none. The checklist shows **Service is complete** only when every role is settled and every listed person has been served.

**Do this now** walks you through it, one gap at a time: **Record the check for the** role, **List the** role, **Serve** a named person, or finish posting and publication.

### Record the check for a role

Open **Record the check for a role**. Pick the **Role**, then a **Result**:

- **Recipients listed**: people exist, and you will add them.
- **None exist**: nobody holds that role. For example, no mortgagee.
- **Same as the owner**: for the taxpayer or the owner's agent.
- **Owner could not be found**: for the owner of record only. It opens the way to posting and publication.

Add **What was searched** and **Date checked**. This is the town's record of the effort.

### Add a recipient

Choose **Add a recipient**. Give the **Role**, **Name**, **Address**, and where the name came from, such as the town's assessing data, a registry book and page, or a site visit.

## Record service

Choose **Record service** under the order, or follow **Do this now**. Fill in:

- **Recipient**, or **No one person: posting or publication** for a posting or a newspaper notice.
- **Date served**.
- **Method**: **Handed to the recipient**, **Left at the place of abode**, **Other method allowed for a civil summons**, **Posted on the property** or **Published in a newspaper**.
- **Served by**, with their name and what they are: **Sheriff**, **Deputy sheriff**, **Local police officer**, **Constable** or **Other**. Hand delivery needs a sheriff, deputy, local police officer or constable.
- **Proof of service**, an uploaded document.

Upload the proof first with **Upload the proof of service**, then choose it in the form. Proof is a return of service, an affidavit of posting, or a newspaper tear sheet. Chiban will not record service without it.

Posting and newspaper publication are allowed only after the owner is marked **Owner could not be found**. They are repeated until the checklist shows **Posting and publication complete** with a date. The order's **Service events** list shows each event with a link to its proof.

## Answer periods

Under **Answer periods**, each served person has a line with the day they were served, the last day to answer, and how many days are left or how long ago it passed. The page prints the counting rule it used, including how a last day on a weekend or holiday moves to the next business day. The earliest running period shows in the **Due** column of the case list and in **Do this now**, as **Wait for the answer**.

If someone answers, open **Record an answer**. Choose **Who answered**, the **Date received** and the document. An answer means the town cannot ask the court to enforce the order itself. **Do this now** then says **Refer the case to town counsel**, because counsel takes an answered order to court. See [court, counsel and penalty exposure](./court-counsel-and-penalties.md).

## When the owner appeals to the zoning board

If the owner appeals to the Zoning Board of Adjustment, open **Record an appeal** under the appeals section. Choose **What is appealed** (the order or the notice of violation), enter the **Appellant** and **Date filed**.

While the appeal is open the case shows **Stayed**, and **Do this now** reads **Wait for the ZBA decision** with a warning that enforcement is stayed. During a stay you can still add notes, photos, service and answers. You cannot issue an order, file the motion to enforce or record a fine. The list's **Appeal stay** column shows which cases are stayed.

When the board decides, record the decision with **Decision** (**Upheld**, **Modified**, **Annulled** or **Withdrawn**) and **Date decided**. The appeals section also has forms to certify imminent peril, record a restraining order, and mark a stay dissolved.
