# Resolving and closing a case

A case ends when the problem is fixed or when the town decides there never was one. Chiban records which.

## The problem was fixed

On each finding, open **More** and choose **Mark corrected**. Enter **Date corrected** and an optional **Note**. When every finding on an order is corrected, Chiban marks the order **Complied with**.

When every finding is settled, **Do this now** reads **Resolve the case**. Choose **Resolve the case**, also under **More actions** at the top of the case. The case moves to **Resolved**. A case in court is ready to resolve once any fine is paid.

## The finding was wrong

Open **More** on the finding and choose **Withdraw**. **Why it is withdrawn** is required. A finding that was already in a notice or order stays on record as withdrawn.

- If every finding is withdrawn and nothing was sent or issued, the case goes back to **Investigating** by itself.
- If something was sent or issued, **Do this now** reads **Record no violation**. Choose it and confirm. The case ends at **No violation found**, and from there it can only be closed.
- An open draft order that includes a withdrawn finding must be discarded and redrafted.

## Close the case

A **Resolved** or **No violation found** case shows **Close the case**. Choose it, enter the **Reason for closing**, and confirm. The case then shows **Closed**, with the date, and cannot be moved to another status.

The demo has no resolved or closed case yet, so the case list's **Closed** tab starts empty. Resolve and close work as described here on a real case.

## What the public site shows

A case starts private to town staff. To show it, open **Visibility** and choose **Show the case on the public site**, then write a **Public summary**. The public page for the parcel then shows the case number, status, dates, the summary, the public complaint text, the provisions cited and the numbers of issued orders. Photos and other documents stay private until you show them one at a time.

Each complaint on the case needs public text first. Under **Complaints**, **Write the public text** and remove anything that could identify the complainant. Chiban refuses to publish while any complaint has none. To take a case off the site, choose **Hide the case from the public site**.

**See what the public sees** on the case opens the public page.
