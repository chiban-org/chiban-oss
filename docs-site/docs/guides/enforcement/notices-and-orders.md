# Notices of violation and cease and desist orders

Once a finding is on record, the town writes to the owner. The first letter is a notice of violation. If the problem is not fixed, the town issues a cease and desist order, the document that can later go to court.

## Send a notice of violation

On a case at **Violation found**, **Do this now** reads **Send a notice of violation**. The form asks for:

- **Findings to include**, the findings the notice covers.
- **Date sent**, which starts as today.
- **How it was sent**: **Handed to the recipient**, **First class mail**, **Certified mail**, **Posted on the property** or **Email**.

Choose **Send notice of violation**. Chiban writes the notice as a PDF and files it under **Documents and photos** as **Notice of violation for** the case number. The notice is listed under **Notices of violation** with how and when it was sent, and **Open notice** shows it.

Chiban does not mail or email the notice. Send it the way you chose, then record the date.

## Record when it was received

The date the owner got the notice sets when penalties start counting: the page notes that **Penalty accrual starts when written notice is received.** When the notice has no receipt date, **Do this now** asks you to record it. Open **Record receipt** and enter **Date received** and **How receipt is known**, such as a signed certified mail receipt.

## Draft a cease and desist order

When the notice is out, **Do this now** moves to **Draft a cease and desist order**. The same form is under **Draft an order** in the orders section. Fill in:

- **Findings the order is based on**, the open findings it covers. A finding already withdrawn or marked corrected cannot go in.
- **Corrective action required**, what the owner must do.
- **Corrective action due**, the date it must be done by.
- **The activity must stop immediately**, ticked when the order demands an immediate stop.

Choose **Draft order**. The order is numbered, for example CD-2026-0001, and kept as a draft. A draft can be changed with **Save the draft**, or thrown away with **Discard the draft**. Its number stays on record as discarded.

Findings about the building code cannot share an order with other kinds of finding. If you mix them, Chiban says **These findings mix building code and other violations. Issue a separate order for each kind.**

## Issue the order

A draft shows **Issue order** with an **Issue date**, starting as today. Only a person on record as a designated enforcement officer on the issue date can issue it. Anyone else gets a message that they are not on record as designated. An administrator adds officers under **Settings**, **Administration**, **Enforcement officers**.

Chiban refuses to issue the order when:

- the corrective action or any other required part of the order is missing. The message names what is missing;
- the corrective action deadline is before the issue date;
- an appeal has stayed enforcement;
- the case is already in court, resolved or closed, or has no finding yet;
- the notice of violation has not been sent. The message reads **Send the notice of violation first. An order can be issued only after the notice of violation is sent.** You can draft the order first, but it cannot be issued until the notice has been sent;
- a finding on the draft was withdrawn or marked corrected after you drafted it. Discard the draft and draft a new one.

When it works, the case moves to **Order issued**, and the order PDF appears under **Documents and photos** as **Cease and desist order** with its number. The order block shows what it requires, the date issued and who issued it, with **Open order**.

After the order is issued, see [serving the order and handling answers](./service-and-answers.md).
