# What applicants see

Applicants and contractors have their own inspections page, with less on it than yours. Knowing what it shows helps you answer their calls.

![The inspections page an applicant sees, with a failed result and a reinspection pending](/img/shots/inspectors/applicant-view.png)

## Their list

**Your inspections** shows one row per inspection with a status:

- **Requested** once they ask for it.
- **Scheduled**, with the day and time, once you book it.
- **Result pending** once the date has passed with no result recorded.
- **Passed** or **Failed** once you record it, with your **Notes for the permit holder**.
- **Cancelled**.

They do not see the checklist, the item-by-item outcomes, your photos or the inspector's name. Your notes are all they get, so write them for someone who was not at the site.

## What they can do

- **Request an inspection**, choosing the type, a preferred day, a contact and access notes. The days offered are the days the town inspects. The request waits on your board marked **Portal**.
- **Cancel this request** on a request they made online, while it is still requested or scheduled. Inspections the office took by phone or at the counter are not theirs to cancel.
- **Request a reinspection** on a failed inspection that has none yet.

They can request only when the permit is issued and not under appeal. Under appeal the page says **Inspections are paused while this permit is under appeal.**

An administrator can turn online requests off. The page then says **Online requests are turned off** and gives the office's phone hours. Staff keep taking requests by phone and at the counter. See [scheduling inspections](./scheduling.md).

## Emails

When you book or cancel an inspection, or record a result, the people on the permit receive an email. The scheduling email says someone should be at the site.
