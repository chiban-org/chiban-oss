# Recording a result

You are standing at the site and the work is done or it is not. This page covers what to enter in Chiban so the permit holder gets a clear answer and the permit moves on.

Open the inspection from **My day** with **Record result**, from the **Results owed** list, or from the permit. The page shows the checklist under **Do this now**.

![An inspection waiting for its result, with a checklist and photo section](/img/shots/inspectors/record-result.png)

## When you can record

A result can be recorded on or after the scheduled date, never before. Until then the page shows the checklist as a preview, with **Record the result on the day**.

Only the inspector the inspection is assigned to can record it. Someone who holds both the land use staff and inspector roles can record any inspection. Anyone else sees **Waiting on** the inspector's name.

Chiban also refuses while the permit is under appeal. The page says inspections are paused and opens the checklist again when the appeal is decided.

## Fill in the checklist

Each item has three choices: **Pass**, **Fail** and **N/A**. Items marked **(optional)** can be left blank.

- A required item cannot be **N/A**. The page says **A required item is N/A. Mark it Pass or Fail.**
- Marking an item **Fail** opens a **Note on** that item. Say what is wrong with it.
- **Notes for the permit holder** is the message the applicant reads. A failed inspection needs at least 10 characters here, and the page says **Write at least 10 characters about what failed.**

A line under the form shows the result Chiban will record: **Result: Passed** when every required item is answered and none failed, **Result: Failed** when any item failed, and **Result: answer the required items first** until you are done. One failed item fails the whole inspection.

On a final inspection a second line says what a pass will do to the permit. See [finals and certificates of occupancy](./finals-and-certificates.md).

## Record it

Choose **Record result**. A confirmation asks, for example, **Record the Final inspection as passed?** and reminds you that **A result cannot be changed once it is recorded.** Choose **Record as passed** or **Record as failed**, or **Keep editing** to go back.

Chiban has no button to edit or undo a recorded result, so read the confirmation before you choose.

After you record it:

- The page shows a **Result** section with each item's outcome and your notes.
- The permit holder is emailed that the inspection has a result.
- The permit's inspection list shows the result, your notes and the number of photos.

## Photos

The **Photos** section on the inspection takes pictures. Open **Add a photo**, choose **Take or choose a photo**, and give it a short title under **What the photo shows**. On a phone this opens the camera. Photos must be JPEG or PNG and no larger than 25 MB.

Photos are for the town's file. The people on the permit do not see them on their inspections page. See [what applicants see](./what-applicants-see.md).
