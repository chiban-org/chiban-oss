# Failed inspections and reinspections

After a failed inspection the contractor fixes the problem and asks for another look. Chiban links the two inspections so you arrive at the reinspection knowing what failed.

## What a failed inspection looks like

The inspection page shows **Failed** with the date, the **Result** section listing each item as **Pass** or **Fail**, your **Notes**, and the photos you attached.

![A failed framing inspection with its result, notes and a photo](/img/shots/inspectors/failed-inspection.png)

## Requesting the reinspection

The **Do this now** card on a failed inspection reads **Request the reinspection**. Choose it and Chiban opens the request form on the schedule board with the permit and the failed inspection already filled in. The only inspection offered is the type that failed. Then take the request and book a time as you would any other. See [scheduling inspections](./scheduling.md).

Applicants and contractors can ask for the reinspection themselves from their inspections page. See [what applicants see](./what-applicants-see.md). Either way the request waits on the board marked **Reinspection**.

A few rules apply:

- Only a failed inspection can be inspected again.
- A failure has one reinspection at a time. While one is requested or scheduled, the failed inspection's card says **Reinspection requested** or **Reinspection scheduled**, with **Open the reinspection**.
- If the reinspection fails too, the newer failure is the one that gets the next reinspection.

## Arriving at the reinspection

On **My day** and on the reinspection itself, a **Last time** note repeats what you wrote when the first inspection failed. The reinspection's page links back to **The failed inspection**, and its progress starts at **Reinspection requested**.

The checklist is the same one, and you fill it in the same way. See [recording a result](./recording-results.md).

## A failed final

A failed final does not close the permit or start a certificate. The permit stays open until a final inspection passes.
