# Working your day

You open Chiban in the morning to find out where you are going and who to call. **My day** answers that. It lists your inspections for one date, in time order, and puts anything you owe a result on at the top.

If you hold the inspector role and no other, the **Inspections** entry in the menu opens **My day** directly. The staff home page, **Today**, also shows how many results you owe and links to the next days that have inspections.

![My day on a desktop screen, listing two scheduled inspections for the day](/img/shots/inspectors/my-day.png)

## What a stop shows

Each stop is one card:

- The start time and the status: **Scheduled**, **Passed**, **Failed**, or **Result owed** once the date has gone by.
- The inspection type, marked **Reinspection** when it follows a failure.
- The site address. Choosing it opens a map.
- The contact's name and phone number. On a phone, choosing the number starts a call.
- **Access** notes the requester left, such as a gate code or a dog in the yard.
- A **Last time** note on a reinspection, with what you wrote when the first inspection failed.
- **Record result** for a stop that is due or owed, **Open the inspection** for one still ahead, or **See the result** for one already recorded. **Permit** opens the permit itself.

Use the arrows beside the date to move to the previous or next day. If you land on a day with nothing booked, the page offers a link to the next day that has inspections. When nothing is booked ahead at all, it links to the waiting requests on the schedule board.

## Still need a result

The **Still need a result** list sits above the stops. It holds your inspections whose date has passed with no result recorded, with the date of each. Choose one to open it and record the result. See [recording a result](./recording-results.md).

The staff home page counts the same list as **Still need a result**. A town-wide view of everything owed, across all inspectors, is on the schedule board. See [scheduling inspections](./scheduling.md).

## On a phone

My day is built for the truck. On a narrow screen the cards stack in one column, the buttons are large enough to hit with a thumb, and the phone numbers and addresses are links.

![My day on a phone, one inspection card per stop](/img/shots/inspectors/my-day-phone.png)

The photo control on an inspection opens the phone's camera. See [recording a result](./recording-results.md).

## When you work more than one calendar

A person who holds both the land use staff and inspector roles, such as the building official, sees an **Inspector** list at the top of My day. Choose a name to see that inspector's day. Staff who are not inspectors are asked to pick an inspector first.
