# The records custodian's work

A records custodian looks after the town's records once they exist. In Chiban that means answering Right-to-Know requests, redacting documents, keeping an eye on the retention schedule, approving disposal, placing legal holds and making sure every document has an archival copy. In a small town the custodian is often also the clerk, so the same person may hold the [land use staff role](../../start-here/staff-roles.md) too.

## Where each job lives

The custodian's staff menu has **Preservation**, **Legal holds**, **Right-to-Know**, **Retention** and **Data export**. **Disposed records** has no menu entry. Open it from the **Retention** page.

| The job | The page | Who can do it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A request arrives and needs a first response | [Log and answer a Right-to-Know request](./rtk-requests.md) | Land use staff and records custodians |
| Records are ready to go to the requester | [Release records and close the request](./rtk-release.md) | Land use staff and records custodians |
| A document has to be shown with parts blacked out | [Redact a document](./redaction.md) | Land use staff and records custodians |
| Records are old enough to dispose of | [Retention and disposal](./retention-and-disposal.md) | Land use staff and custodians propose a batch. Only a custodian approves it. |
| Litigation or a dispute means records must be kept | [Legal holds](./legal-holds.md) | Administrators and records custodians |
| A document has no archival copy | [Archival copies](./preservation.md) | Land use staff, administrators and records custodians |

The [roles and permissions reference](../../reference/roles-and-permissions.mdx) lists every permission and the roles that have it. **Data export** is covered in [the export archive format](../../reports-and-export/export-format.md).

## Trying these pages in the demo

The demo has no records custodian sign-in. Most of this section can be tried as Sam Clerk, who holds the land use staff role: the Right-to-Know log, redaction, the retention list, proposing a batch and the preservation list. **Legal holds** opens as Avery Admin. Approving a disposition batch needs the custodian role, so nobody can do that step in the demo.
