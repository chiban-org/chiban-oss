# Redact a document

Redact a document when part of it cannot be shown, such as a private telephone number in a letter. Chiban never changes the original. It makes a new copy with the covered areas removed, and the copy stays private until someone approves it. Land use staff and records custodians can redact. Chiban redacts PDF, JPEG and PNG files only.

## Start from the right place

For a Right-to-Know request, open the request, search for the document and choose **Redact for this request**. For any other document, open the record it belongs to, choose **Manage** on the document, then **Redact this document**.

## Draw the boxes

1. Under **The copy is for**, choose **The public website** or **A Right-to-Know request**. If you came from a request, it is already set to the request.
2. Drag on a page to draw a box over what must go, or choose **Add a box to page** for a box you can move. Long files show twelve pages at a time.
3. For every box, choose an **Exemption**. If you choose **Made confidential by another law**, enter the **Law that makes it confidential**.
4. Under **How the exemption applies**, write at least 20 characters.
5. Use the move and resize buttons to nudge a box, or **Remove this box**.
6. Choose **Make the redacted copy**.

Chiban needs at least one box and will not make the copy while any box lacks an exemption or explanation. If it cannot finish, it asks you to check that every box is on the page and try again.

Everything under a box is removed from the copy, text included, not just covered. For a PDF, Chiban also clears the file's hidden information and checks the result.

## Approve the copy

Making the copy releases nothing. It stays private until someone with the right permission approves it.

- A copy for a request is approved in the release step on the request page. See [Release records and close the request](./rtk-release.md).
- A copy for the public website is approved in the Documents section of the record's page. Under the document, choose the copy in **Version to approve**, write what you checked under **What you checked before approving**, and choose **Approve for the public**. This takes the permission to show documents on the public site, which land use staff, administrators and records custodians have. A custodian can therefore make a redacted copy and publish it without handing it to someone else. Without the permission, the page says a staff member who has it must approve the copy.
