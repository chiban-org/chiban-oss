# Legal holds

A legal hold keeps records from being disposed of, whatever the retention schedule says. Place one when litigation, a dispute or a records request means something must be kept. Administrators and records custodians can place and release holds.

## What a hold covers

A hold on a project, an application, a document, an enforcement case or a Right-to-Know request blocks disposal of everything under it. A hold also protects a record already in a proposed batch: when the batch is approved, that record is skipped and marked **Skipped: a legal hold was placed**. A document under a hold shows a **Legal hold** label in its record's document list.

## Place a hold

1. Open **Legal holds** and choose **Place a hold**.
2. Under **Hold on**, choose **Project**, **Application**, **Document**, **Enforcement case** or **Right-to-Know request**.
3. Enter the **Application number, case number, request number, project title, or document title**. Use the number when you can: if more than one record has the same name, Chiban asks for it. A document that has already been disposed of cannot be held.
4. Give a **Reason (at least 10 characters)** and choose **Place hold**.

An application's own page has a **Place a legal hold** option under **Records**, for the same job.

## Review and release holds

The **Active** and **Released** tabs list holds newest first, with each one's subject, kind, reason and placement date.

To release one, open **Release** on its row, enter a **Release reason (at least 10 characters)** and choose **Release hold**, then confirm. The row moves to **Released** with the date and your reason. The records are no longer protected by that hold. If another hold still covers a record, that hold keeps it, and the **Retention** page shows the count when a record has more than one.
